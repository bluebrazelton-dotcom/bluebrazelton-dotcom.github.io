Syllabus Date Shifter

Roll a syllabus or course schedule forward to a new term. Dates move by course week and weekday, so "Week 3, Tuesday" stays Week 3, Tuesday. Your file is processed in this browser and is never uploaded.

1Choose your file

A Word file (.docx) comes back with its formatting intact. A PDF can be read, but the result comes back as a new, plain Word file without the original layout.

Drop a .docx or .pdf here, or press Enter to choose a file

2Set the old and new terms

Use the first day of classes for each term. Any day in Week 1 works, because both terms are lined up by their Week 1 Monday.

Break weeks and no-class days (recommended)

Week numbers, comma-separated. Spring break in Week 10 → "10".

Instructional weeks are matched around breaks: old Week 11 becomes the 10th class week of the new term.

Holidays, reading days, and so on. Any date that lands on one is flagged for you to fix by hand.

3Review every change

Uncheck anything that isn't a course date (a citation year, a policy effective date). Edit the new text directly if you need to.
