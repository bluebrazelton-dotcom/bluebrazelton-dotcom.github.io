Nursing Exam Tools

Four small tools for building and reviewing teacher-made exams. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing you paste or type is sent anywhere.

Item analysis helper

Paste item statistics exported from ExamSoft, Canvas, or a spreadsheet. Columns are matched by name: Item, Difficulty (p-value), Point biserial (or discrimination), and optionally Key plus the share of students choosing each option (A, B, C…). Use item numbers only, never student names.

Flags are prompts to look, not verdicts. A very easy item on essential safety content may be exactly right, and on small classes (under about 30 students) these statistics swing a lot.

Test blueprint builder

Enter how many items fall in each cell. Totals, percentages, and coverage flags update as you type. Start from the NCLEX-RN Client Needs categories, or switch to your own course objectives.

NCLEX-RN target ranges are from NCSBN's 2026 RN Test Plan (effective April 2026), where the percentages are unchanged from 2023. A single course exam doesn't need to mirror the NCLEX; edit the targets to fit the course.

Exam timing calculator

Enter how many of each item type the exam has. Time per item is editable: the defaults are starting points, not a standard.

Prototype assumption: the per-item seconds and the program-level adjustment (+15% early, −10% final semester) are placeholders for faculty to set. Research on NGN items finds that complex item types take longer and that students need more time early in a curriculum, but it doesn't give one standard rate.

NGN unfolding case builder

A guided outline for writing a six-item unfolding case study, with one item for each step of the NCSBN Clinical Judgment Measurement Model. Fill in what you have; the draft builds below and can be copied or downloaded.
