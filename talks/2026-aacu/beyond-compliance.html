Beyond Compliance: Six-Indicator Self-Assessment

Your AI governance framework already tells faculty, students, and peers whether your institution is reacting to this moment or building the capacity to adapt. Use this page to read it the way they do.

How to use it

  1. Open your institution's public AI framework (policy, guidelines, and any linked standards).
  2. For each indicator, compare what you find with the early and maturing signs, then mark where it sits: Early, Developing, or Maturing.
  3. Pick one indicator to change in your next revision, and name who needs to be in the room to change it.

Two things to keep in mind

  • Relabeling a default without building behind it changes the signal, not the substance. Prohibition is not the problem; what separates frameworks is what gets built behind the default.
  • Maturing is never complete. Kane and colleagues use “maturing,” not “mature,” because “the process is never complete” (Kane et al., 2019, p. 46). A gap is a starting point, not a grade.
