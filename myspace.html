Blue's Blurbs

About me:

I'm an Associate Professor at Northern Arizona University, where my work sits where higher education, organizational theory, leadership, and technology all bump into each other. Short version: I'm a practitioner-scholar who thinks human-centered organizations are the only ones that can effectively implement opportunity technologies.

These days that mostly means AI in higher education: how institutions adopt generative tools without losing the parts of academic life worth keeping. I'm running a multi-phase AI governance study, writing a book on AI frameworks and instructional design with Susan Purrington (under contract with Second Draft Labs), and building Aegis / MAPS, a working system for AI-supported faculty work.

I came up through student affairs before faculty work, so I can't write as if the gap between a journal article and a Tuesday in a real office doesn't exist. I've chaired 17 dissertations and sat on 15 more, and I teach with a lot of humor.

Who I'd like to meet:

Anyone at the AAC&U Conference on AI and Higher Education who is working on AI governance, faculty productivity, or institutional digital maturity, or who just wants to compare notes. I'll be in person in Atlanta, Oct 28–30, and presenting both of my sessions on Zoom:

Reclaim your time, reclaim your agency (AI as productivity infrastructure for the professoriate) and Beyond compliance (reading AI governance as a signal of institutional digital maturity).
