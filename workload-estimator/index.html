How the estimates work

Reading

Pages per hour = (words per minute × 60) ÷ words per page. Reading speeds by purpose and difficulty:

Writing time is assumed to grow in a straight line with length. Reflection takes half as long as argument, and research takes twice as long. The original authors call the writing estimates far more speculative than the reading estimates.

ABOR benchmark

ABOR Policy 2-224 sets one unit of credit at 45 hours of work, where an hour is 50 minutes of class time or 60 minutes of independent work. The status shows "on target" within ±10% of 45 × credits.

Assumptions added for this prototype

  • Media time multipliers (×1, ×1.5, ×2).
  • Discussion posts: text at 333 words per hour; audio/video at 3× runtime.
  • Exams: 5 study hours per exam by default.
  • Term-length presets.

These are starting values, not research findings. Change them to fit your course.
