The Patience Game — loop on one page

The one-page brief written on October 13, 2026, at the start of Build 2, published as written. It calls itself the whole brief for the first slice.

v0.1 · 2026-10-13 · decided in conversation; this page is the whole brief for the first slice. Working title only. Everything a builder needs is here. Nothing from the previous build's folder is in scope.

What it is

A browser incremental about an academic career, high school to legacy. Numbers go up; you buy things that make them go up faster; the career is the ladder. Tone: cartoon academia — setbacks are a two-second animation, then you're back at the desk. The first ten minutes stand alone as a complete game.

The loop (one engine, every chapter)

click a station → earn the chapter currency → buy upgrades → upgrades improve clicks, unlock new stations, or automate → reach the chapter's conversion threshold → convert the pile into a little of the next tier → new chapter, new art, same engine.

Currencies — Option B+D

  • One currency per chapter (tiered). Homework → Credits → Data/Notes → Pages → Applications → Dossier → Citations (names provisional). Leaving a chapter converts the pile at a brutal rate ("4,312 Homework → 1 Acceptance Letter"). Earlier tiers keep trickling in the background.
  • Path currency (D) appears at Grad school when the path does: Papers (social science) / Grants (environmental science) / Archive Days (history) / Citations-to-you (law). Earned by one path-specific station. Flavor and names differ by path; costs and curves do not.
  • Patience — the scarce resource. A refilling tank, not a currency. Clicks and wait-timers drain it; time and upgrades refill it. At zero you can still click, but output degrades (Rushed/Uneven) — never a wall. Chapter 1: currency is cheap, Patience is the only brake. Late game the label flips to their Patience (committee, editor, dean).

Stations

  • Every chapter opens with exactly one station (the chapter's effort verb). More stations are upgrades — buy one, a new button appears.
  • Four button rhythms, no mini-games: Tap (fast, small, costs Patience) · Hold (fill a bar, bigger payout) · Cooldown (one click, timer, often refills Patience) · Timer (submit, wait, verdict).
  • Each station = art + verb + rhythm + what it earns. Chapter 1 has ≤3; late chapters ≤4.

Chapters (7) and target active minutes

1 High school (8) · 2 Undergrad (10) · 3 Grad school (12) · 4 Dissertation (15) · 5 Job market (12) · 6 Tenure track (18) · 7 After tenure / legacy (15). ≈ 90 min active, spread over sittings. Offline progress on, capped at ~8 h; cap grows with later upgrades. Wait-timers scale: seconds in Ch.1, minutes by Ch.6.

Path choice

At the exit of Chapter 2 ("Declare" — four envelopes). Ch.1–2 are fully generic. Optional Ch.2 elective upgrades are flavor-only and get a one-line callback on the Declare screen. No branching logic before Ch.3.

Chapter 1 — the slice (build this first)

  • Station: Study (pencil + notebook, Tap). Earns Homework.
  • Upgrades, in order: Planner (+per click) → Library Card (unlocks Read: book, Hold, bigger payout, cheaper Patience) → Highlighters (× Read) → A Club (unlocks Show Up: Cooldown, refills Patience) → Teacher Who Noticed (first idle trickle) → repeatable tiers of each.
  • Cost curve: base × 1.15^n per repeat purchase. First purchase ≤30 s; something new every 2–3 min; chapter done at 8–10 min.
  • Exit: threshold of Homework → Acceptance Letter. One conversion screen, one joke.
  • Done when: a person plays ten minutes without instruction and wants chapter 2.

Tone rules

  1. Setbacks are animations, not mechanics: nothing bought is ever taken back; only Patience dips.
  2. Humor lives in upgrade flavor text (one dry line each) and conversion screens; mechanics text stays plain.
  3. Punch at situations, never people or institutions. Reviewer 2 is the only villain (a job title, not a person). No real institutions.
  4. Self-aware, not cynical: say the joke once ("Refills slowly. Like everything else."), then stop. Never readable as "don't do this career."
  5. Setbacks are scheduled, not random: the timer is visible, the rejection comes anyway.

Not in the slice (parked)

Mini-games · running-between-stations mechanics beyond cooldowns · second currency · any path content · chapters 2–7 · save/export beyond localStorage · sound.

Build notes

Single HTML file, plain JS, no framework; localStorage save; hosted on the creator's site. Balance in a spreadsheet first (cost, income, minutes-to-next-unlock). Pixel art: one consistent sprite size; creator supplies or approves the first button before the agent generates more. Agent stops and shows the slice before touching chapter 2.
