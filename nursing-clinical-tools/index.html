Clinical Teaching Tools

Four small tools for the clinical side of a nursing course. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is saved or sent anywhere, so copy or download what you want to keep.

Dosage-calculation problem generator

Generates fresh practice or quiz problems with a worked answer key. The same version code always gives the same set, so Version A and Version B can be rebuilt any time.

Problem types
Rounding used: weight to the nearest tenth of a kg; tablets to the nearest half tablet; oral and IV volumes to the nearest tenth of a mL; pump rates to the nearest tenth of a mL/hr; drip rates to the nearest whole gtt/min. Change the wording if your program's rounding policy differs. Drug names are deliberately generic, so swap in course drugs and check that every dose is realistic before using a set.

    Clinical and simulation hours tracker

    Track each student's hours against the course requirement, including how much simulation counts toward it.

    Use initials or student ID numbers, never names. Nothing is stored: when you close the page the data is gone, so download the CSV to keep it. The simulation ratio and cap are placeholders; set them from your program's policy and Board of Nursing rules.

    Clinical rotation grid builder

    Rotates clinical groups through sites in equal blocks, so every group gets every site once and no site gets two groups in the same block.

    Clinical feedback comment bank

    Pick a competency and a performance level, then click a starter sentence to add it to your draft. Replace the bracketed parts with what you actually saw. Specific examples are what make feedback useful.

    Competencies follow the QSEN framework plus professionalism. Replace these starters with your program's own clinical evaluation tool language before faculty use it.

    Prototype built for nursing faculty. Generated problems, ratios, and comment starters are drafts for faculty review, not validated instruments.
